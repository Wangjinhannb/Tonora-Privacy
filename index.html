适用范围

本政策适用于 Tonora Android 应用（软件包名称：com.tonora.app），由 Tonora 开发者（GitHub: Wangjinhannb）提供。Tonora 是一款离线音乐练习工具，包含乐器调音、节拍器、标准音、目标跟唱及实时音高监测。

1. 麦克风与音频处理

当您主动启动调音、跟唱或实时音高监测并授予麦克风权限后，应用使用麦克风采集声音，在您的设备上实时分析音高与输入电平。音频信号仅用于这些功能，不保存为录音文件，不上传至服务器，也不提供给开发者或第三方。音高曲线和练习统计在当前练习会话内存中处理。停止拾音或离开相关功能时，应用停止使用麦克风。您可随时通过 Android 系统设置撤销权限。

2. 本地设置与数据收集

Tonora 无需账号或登录，不使用广告或分析 SDK，不请求互联网访问权限。基准频率、音量、节奏、主题和其他应用偏好存储于设备本地，用于恢复您的设置。节拍器可在您启动后台播放后继续发声，并通过系统通知提供控制。通知权限仅用于相关播放通知，不用于广告或营销。

应用不会向开发者收集或传输个人信息、音频、练习记录或应用设置。开发者不出售或共享这些数据，不在服务器保存用户资料。

3. 数据保留、删除与安全

音频分析与实时练习数据仅在当前会话中处理，不建立云端录音或练习档案。本地设置保留至您更改设置、清除应用数据或卸载应用。您可在 Android 设置中管理权限并清除本地数据。应用不提供账号，因此无需申请删除账号或服务器端用户数据。应用禁用 Android 标准应用备份；设备系统与制造商的独立迁移或备份行为受其各自设置和政策约束。

4. 第三方服务

Android 系统和 Google Play 可能独立处理安装、设备、应用诊断等相关信息，其处理行为适用各自的隐私政策。此隐私政策网页由 GitHub Pages 托管，访问网页时，托管服务可能处理通常的网络请求和访问日志。请参阅 GitHub 隐私声明。本网页不额外嵌入广告、分析脚本或追踪器。

5. 联系方式

如对 Tonora 的隐私实践有疑问，请通过 Google Play 商店详情页 所列开发者联系邮箱进行联系，也可通过 Tonora 隐私反馈渠道 提出一般性隐私问题。GitHub 议题是公开的，请勿在其中发布个人敏感信息、录音或其他私密资料。若您主动联系开发者，您提供的联系方式及内容仅用于处理和回复您的请求，并在处理完成且不再需要时删除；法律另有要求的除外。

6. 政策更新

如果应用的隐私实践发生变化，将更新本网页和应用内的隐私说明，并在此标明更新日期。

Scope

This policy applies to Tonora for Android (package name: com.tonora.app), provided by the Tonora developer (GitHub: Wangjinhannb). Tonora is an offline music practice tool offering instrument tuning, a metronome, reference tones, target-pitch singing practice and live pitch monitoring.

1. Microphone and audio processing

When you intentionally start tuning, singing practice or live pitch monitoring and grant microphone permission, Tonora uses the microphone to analyze pitch and input level in real time on your device. Audio is used only for these features. The app does not save audio recordings, upload audio to servers or transmit it to the developer or third parties. Pitch graphs and practice statistics are processed in memory during the current session. Microphone use stops when you stop listening or leave the relevant feature. You can revoke microphone permission in Android settings at any time.

2. Local settings and data collection

Tonora does not require accounts or sign-in, use advertising or analytics SDKs, or request Internet access permission. Reference frequency, volume, rhythm, theme and other preferences are stored locally to restore your settings. The metronome can continue playing in the background when you start it, with controls in a system notification. Notification permission is used for related playback notifications, not advertising or marketing.

The app does not collect or transmit personal information, audio, practice records or settings to the developer. The developer does not sell or share such data or maintain server-side user profiles.

3. Retention, deletion and security

Audio analysis and live practice data are processed during the current session without creating cloud recordings or practice archives. Local preferences remain until changed, cleared or removed by uninstalling the app. You can manage permissions and clear local app data in Android settings. There is no app account or server-side user data to request deletion of. The app disables standard Android app backup; independent device migration or backup behavior provided by the operating system or device manufacturer is governed by their settings and policies.

4. Third-party services

Android and Google Play may independently process installation, device and app diagnostic information under their respective privacy policies. This privacy policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process ordinary network requests and access logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page does not embed additional ads, analytics scripts or trackers.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the email address listed on Tonora's Google Play listing, or use the Tonora privacy feedback channel for general privacy questions. GitHub issues are public; please do not post sensitive personal information, recordings or other private material there. If you choose to contact the developer, information you provide is used to handle and respond to your request and deleted when no longer needed, unless retention is required by law.

6. Policy changes

If the app's privacy practices change, this website and the in-app privacy notice will be updated, and the revision date will be shown here.
